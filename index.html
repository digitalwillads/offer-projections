The $5,000 offer, in numbers

We give a new client $2,000 of ad spend and the first month of work free. To them that is $5,000 of value. It costs us $2,600 when it works and $1,100 when we bail early. If it works, they pay $9,000 upfront for the next 3 months.

$9,000 in, minus $1,350 commission, minus 3 months of servicing at $400

Assumptions

Marketing spend is what it costs to bring in one new trial, paid in the month the trial starts. Clients that stay pay one third of the commit price each month, and each month after that the group shrinks by the churn rate. A trial that will not work stops early: we spend the bail amount on ads, not the full ad spend. The free month costs the labor amount either way. Every month after that costs the ongoing amount. Sales commission is paid in the month the client pays the commit. Per the 2026-08-04 structure it is 25% of the first paid month plus 10% of months 2 and 3, which is 15% of a $9,000 commit; on a sale Will closes it is 17%. No commission after month 3.
